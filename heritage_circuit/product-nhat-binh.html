Thông Tin
  • Nhật Bình was the ceremonial court dress of queens, princesses, and high-ranking women of the Nguyễn Dynasty, worn from the 19th into the early 20th century.
  • The name comes from its square, front-facing collar — cổ vuông — which sits flat like the character for "sun" (nhật) when viewed head-on.
  • Colour marked rank at court: bright yellow was reserved for the queen, red for princesses and senior consorts, with purple and blue worn further down the hierarchy.
  • The roundels on the chest and back are embroidered with phượng, the phoenix — the female counterpart to the dragon roundels reserved for the emperor's robe.
  • The rounded headdress seen with it, khăn vành, is wound from a single long strip of fabric, its brim sloping gently at the back like a crescent moon.
  • This reproduction keeps the hand-embroidered collar and phoenix roundels, tailored for modern brides and ceremony wear rather than daily palace duty.
